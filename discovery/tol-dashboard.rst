.. _tol-dashboard:

======================
Tree of Life Dashboard
======================

The Tree of Life (ToL) [#f1]_ dashboard provides an overview of data
statistics and a worldview of the Genome Acquisition Labs (GAL)s and partners
associated with data submissions.

Explore the dashboard on the
`Tree of Life dashboard page <tol-dashboard-page_>`__ . Limited information
is displayed if you are not logged in.

.. hint::

   * Click a card item on the dashboard for additional details.

   * Click a location on the world map to view more details about a GAL or
     partner.

the world map.

A popup dialog will be displayed information about the GAL or Partner such as
name, state, country and number of samples produced.

Tree of Life Inspection
--------------------------

A `ToL inspection <tol-inspect-page_>`__ analyses sample metadata and
facilitates search faceting as well as data discovery of the results.
