Submitting Spatial Transcriptomics Images
-----------------------------------------

Spatial Transcriptomics image submissions use the Fluorescence In Situ
Hybridisation (FISH) standard to describe the
experimental context, imaging parameters, probe information and spatial
relationships required to interpret gene
expression patterns within tissue sections.
